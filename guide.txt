🧠 *AI & PERSONALITY COMMANDS*
• Direct DM Chat : Just text the bot directly with no prefix!
• !ai <prompt> : Explicitly ask Gemini a question.
  └ Example: !ai What are the main rules of basketball?
• !talk <prompt> : Get AI responses back as a Voice Note.
  └ Example: !talk Tell me a funny joke
• !analyze : Reply to any message to analyze tone & context.
• !analyzechat : Complete chat history analytics.
• !describe : Send/reply to a photo for AI vision inspection.
• !mode <persona> : Change AI tone (normal, friend, roast, tutor, hype).

🎨 *MEDIA & CREATIVE COMMANDS*
• !imagine <prompt> : Generate AI artwork from text.
  └ Example: !imagine futuristic sports car in space
• !song <title/artist> : Download MP3 audio.
  └ Example: !song Drake Passionfruit
• !video <title> : Download MP4 video.
  └ Example: !video Barcelona vs Real Madrid highlights
• !sticker : Send/reply to an image to convert to sticker.
• !meme <top | bottom> : Overlay meme text onto photos.
  └ Example: !meme When the code works | on the first try
• !tts <text> : Convert text to custom audio voice note.

⚽ *SPORTS & UTILITIES*
• !match <team> : Live scores & upcoming match status.
  └ Example: !match Barcelona
• !summarize : Reply to long text for a bullet point summary.
• !wiki <topic> : Fetch Wikipedia facts & summary.
• !tr <lang> <text> : Instant translation.
  └ Example: !tr Spanish Good morning my friend
• !remind <mins> <text> : Set a timer reminder notification.
  └ Example: !remind 10 Check the food in the oven
• !trivia : Play interactive quiz questions.
• !search <query> : Search saved chat logs.
• !calc <expr> : Fast math calculation engine.
  └ Example: !calc (450 * 2) / 5 + 10
• !ping / !status : Check bot uptime & key rotation status.

👥 *GROUP TOOLS*
• !tagall <msg> : Mention all group members visibly.
• !hidetag <msg> : Send invisible mass ping notification.

🔓 *AUTOMATION & CONTROLS*
• !Neymar : Sleep / Wake bot toggle.
• View Once Saver : Auto-captures View-Once media.
• Auto Status Viewer & Liker : Automatically views & likes contacts status.
• Anti-Delete Recovery : Auto-reposts deleted messages & media.
